TCP and UDP: two transport approaches

Both can transport information across multiple devices, but their communication characteristics differ.

TCP

CONNECTION-ORIENTED

Formal connection setup and teardown, acknowledgments, retransmission when needed, and flow control support reliable delivery.

UDP

CONNECTIONLESS

No formal setup or teardown, no acknowledgments, no retransmission mechanism, and no TCP-style flow control. It has low overhead.

Multiplexing: different traffic at the same time
